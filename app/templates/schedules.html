Scheduled scans

Recurring, unattended scans of your targets. Results are added to the register automatically as a new scan, so you can track drift over time on the Compare page.

Scheduled scans are network-only by design: credentialed audits need credentials, which Argus never stores, so run those interactively from Run scan. Protected (do-not-scan) assets are always skipped.
